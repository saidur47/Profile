I'm a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Florida State University, where I also work as a graduate teaching assistant, helping run undergraduate and graduate courses, lab sessions, and office hours.

My research sits at the intersection of disaggregated memory and distributed systems. I'm interested in what it takes to coordinate compute and memory once they're no longer bound to the same box, and pooled instead across a data center.

Before starting my PhD, I earned a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in Dhaka, Bangladesh, graduating Magna Cum Laude, and spent several years as a lab instructor teaching databases, computer architecture, and digital logic design.

Current research

My research explores system support for disaggregated memory, with an emphasis on CXL-attached memory pools and distributed resource sharing. I am particularly interested in how operating-system and container mechanisms should evolve when memory is remote, shared, and accessed through a fabric. My current work investigates CXL-based mechanisms for reducing data movement and startup overhead in containerized systems.
